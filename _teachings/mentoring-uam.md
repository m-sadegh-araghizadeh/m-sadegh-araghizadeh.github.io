During my Ph.D., I mentored M.Sc. students working on various UAM configurations (multi-tiltrotor,
side-by-side, coaxial rotor, and quadrotor) under different flight conditions including vortex ring
state, cruising, ascending/descending, hovering, ground effect, and forward flight.
