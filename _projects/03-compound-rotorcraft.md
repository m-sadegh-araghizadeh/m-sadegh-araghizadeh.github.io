Comprehensive aerodynamics and aeroacoustics analysis of a high-speed compound rotorcraft
in cruising flight. The coupled vortex-method framework captures the interaction of the coaxial
rotors with the wing and propellers, quantifying download, trim state, and noise sources across
the cruise envelope. Manuscript submitted to the *AIAA Journal*.
