Investigation of how atmospheric turbulence inflow degrades the aerodynamic and acoustic performance
of side-by-side eVTOL configurations. Synthetic turbulence is generated and convected through the
coupled vortex-method solver to quantify fluctuations in rotor loads and noise.

The study provides guidance for flight-envelope definition and ride-comfort assessment of UAM
aircraft operating in real-world, gusty environments. Manuscript submitted to *Physics of Fluids*.
