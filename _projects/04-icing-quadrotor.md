Icing simulations of a UAM quadrotor in full configuration using a hybrid Eulerian-Lagrangian
approach: droplet impingement and collection efficiency are computed with an Eulerian method,
while ice accretion and its aerodynamic impact on the rotors are evaluated with coupled
vortex and particle-based methods.

Published in the *AIAA Journal* (2025) together with collaborators at Gyeongsang National University.
