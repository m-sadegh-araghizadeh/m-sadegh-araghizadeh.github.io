Master's research project at the Research Institute of Aviation Technology, Ferdowsi University of
Mashhad, under Prof. Jalil Rezaeepazhand. The project covered parametric propeller geometry
modeling, blade aerodynamic analysis, and performance optimization of propellers for small aircraft.
