Ph.D. project on the aerodynamic and aeroacoustic performance of side-by-side Urban Air Mobility
aircraft in full configuration, including the influence of ground effect. The work couples nonlinear
vortex methods (UVLM, free-wake, and vortex particle methods) to capture rotor-airframe interaction,
wake evolution, and blade loading.

Key outcomes:

- Journal cover and featured article in *Physics of Fluids* (2024)
- Aeroacoustic footprint and loading analysis in hover and near-ground operation
- In-house coupled vortex-method solver with MPI parallelization
