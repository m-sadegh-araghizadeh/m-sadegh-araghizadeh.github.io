Ongoing project on predicting the vortex ring state (VRS) of a side-by-side UAM configuration with
the vortex particle method. The work focuses on wake topology, unsteady loading, and the interaction
of the two rotors' recirculating wakes near descent conditions.

Presented at the 10th Asian/Australian Rotorcraft Forum (ARF 2024, Melbourne, Australia).
