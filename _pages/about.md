I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Westlake University specializing in computational fluid dynamics (CFD), physics-informed machine learning, and unsteady aerodynamics. My work sits at the intersection of high-performance scientific computing and reduced-order modeling, aiming to bridge classical fluid mechanics with modern artificial intelligence.

> Enable real-time, physics-accurate aerodynamic predictions and design optimization for complex unsteady flows in real-world environments.

My research focuses on developing computational methods for understanding and predicting complex fluid flows, with particular interest in aerodynamics, bio-inspired flight, and emerging aerospace technologies. I am interested in combining physics-based modeling, numerical simulation, and machine learning to develop efficient and reliable tools for engineering applications.

## Research Interests

- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Flow Physics
- Scientific Machine Learning and Reduced-Order Modeling
- Bio-Inspired Flight
- Lagrangian Vortex Methods
- Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI)
- Urban Air Mobility (UAM)
- Clean Energy and Emerging Fluid-Dynamics Applications
